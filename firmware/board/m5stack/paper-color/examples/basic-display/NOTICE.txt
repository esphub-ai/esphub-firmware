M5Stack PaperColor Basic Display
https://docs.m5stack.com/en/arduino/papercolor/display

This EspHub implementation draws the layout described in the M5Stack example.
It uses fonts from the pinned M5GFX dependency. M5GFX and M5Unified carry
the M5Stack MIT notice reproduced in ../../../LICENSE.

M5GFX 0.3.0 font credits
https://github.com/m5stack/M5GFX/blob/28d47949bdc84f30881623476f555398e2194313/README.md#license

FreeSansBold12pt7b and FreeSansBold24pt7b use Adafruit's GFX bitmap format.
https://github.com/m5stack/M5GFX/blob/28d47949bdc84f30881623476f555398e2194313/src/lgfx/Fonts/GFXFF/license.txt
