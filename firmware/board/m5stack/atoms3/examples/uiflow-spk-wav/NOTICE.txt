Derived from the UiFlow2 v2.5.5 AtomS3 Atomic SPK Base speaker example, pinned
at a61a29409f09f1f4bebae05e15c4bd6dcc4956b6. The Python and m5f2 project
describe the same example. SpeakerBase follows the repository MIT license.
https://github.com/m5stack/uiflow-micropython/blob/a61a29409f09f1f4bebae05e15c4bd6dcc4956b6/examples/base/speaker/atoms3_speaker_example.py
https://github.com/m5stack/uiflow-micropython/blob/a61a29409f09f1f4bebae05e15c4bd6dcc4956b6/m5stack/libs/base/speaker.py
https://docs.m5stack.com/en/atom/Atomic_SPK_Base

The Flash WAV is an original synthesized tone stored as a read-only resource
in the OTA app and used for /flash/res/audio/66.wav. Place a PCM WAV of your
choice at the root of a FAT microSD card as 66.wav for the second playback.
The original recording's redistribution rights could not be verified. The
repository recording is absent from the firmware and the simulation fixtures.
