Retains the MIT Echo Base hardware setup from the record/playback port.
The original 66.wav has no verified redistribution provenance. The app
substitutes an original build-generated 16 kHz mono PCM WAV with dual-mono I2S output, under this
repository's MIT license. It resides in app Flash and needs no filesystem
or asset download. The example plays through once, then idles in M5.update(). No upstream recording is distributed.

M5Unified and M5GFX are MIT. ESP-IDF I2S and the driver APIs are Apache-2.0.
