ArduinoHttpClient was not pinned by the sketch. Reviewed ArduinoHttpClient 0.6.0
(6f2659de4c0523f0a5fa4bc1154d1e5dc1b01546), Apache-2.0, MCQN Ltd and Arduino contributors.
https://github.com/arduino-libraries/ArduinoHttpClient/tree/6f2659de4c0523f0a5fa4bc1154d1e5dc1b01546

No source or assets from those libraries are distributed by this port. It uses
ESP-IDF Ethernet (W5500 MACRAW), esp_netif/lwIP and esp_http_client plus the
AtomS3 board's existing M5Unified/M5GFX dependencies.
https://github.com/espressif/esp-idf/tree/v5.5.4/components/esp_eth
https://github.com/espressif/esp-idf/tree/v5.5.4/components/esp_http_client
