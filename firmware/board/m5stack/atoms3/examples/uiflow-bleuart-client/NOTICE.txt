Derived from the AtomS3 BLE UART Client in UiFlow2 v2.5.5, pinned at
a61a29409f09f1f4bebae05e15c4bd6dcc4956b6. The .py and .m5f2 are one example.
https://github.com/m5stack/uiflow-micropython/blob/a61a29409f09f1f4bebae05e15c4bd6dcc4956b6/examples/system/bleuart/atoms3_bleuart_client_example.py
https://github.com/m5stack/uiflow-micropython/blob/a61a29409f09f1f4bebae05e15c4bd6dcc4956b6/examples/system/bleuart/cores3_bleuart_server_example.py
https://github.com/m5stack/uiflow-micropython/tree/a61a29409f09f1f4bebae05e15c4bd6dcc4956b6/m5stack/libs/bleuart

Uses the ESP-IDF v5.5.4 Bluetooth stack and Apache NimBLE (Apache-2.0).
M5Unified 96576a14247ddc935149145aa6b51943464120f1 and M5GFX 0.3.0
are MIT AtomS3 build dependencies. The client uses UART0 for status.
The native build uses ESP32-S3 integrated BLE. The QEMU validation
build uses ESP-IDF's H4 transport over UART1 to a local test controller.
No fixture, firmware binary or media asset is embedded by the example.
